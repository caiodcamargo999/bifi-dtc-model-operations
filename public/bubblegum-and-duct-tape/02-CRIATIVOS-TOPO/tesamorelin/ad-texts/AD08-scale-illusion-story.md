**[SUBHEAD — A APARÊNCIA DE SUCESSO VS. O ESPELHO]**
Over the following months, the scale showed clear progress. My colleagues noticed my face and shoulders looked slimmer, and my routine checkups showed positive trends in standard vital markers.

Yet when I looked in the mirror, an unexpected pattern emerged:

My arms and shoulders had lost noticeable definition and muscular tone, but my central midsection remained a firm, protruding dome that pushed firmly against my waistband.

**[A REVELAÇÃO DO ESPECIALISTA]**
When I discussed this with an endocrinology specialist and reviewed a high-precision body composition scan, the underlying physiology became clear:

During significant calorie deficits, the body often burns easily accessible skeletal muscle tissue while leaving dense visceral fat—the inflammatory tissue stored behind the abdominal wall—locked in place.

My doctor explained that deep visceral fat is not unlocked by calorie restriction alone. It requires natural nocturnal signaling from the pituitary gland through Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH), which naturally declines with age.

**[A RESPOSTA COM O PROTOCOLO GHRH]**
That led me to the clinical research on **Tesamorelin**—a physician-prescribed 44-amino-acid peptide secretagogue designed specifically to reactivate the body's natural nocturnal fat-mobilizing pathways.

In clinical trials measured by CT scans, Tesamorelin demonstrated significant reductions in deep visceral fat volume while actively protecting lean muscle mass from catabolic breakdown.

Under physician supervision through a specialized telehealth protocol, targeting the endocrine mechanism made all the difference in restoring a balanced body composition.
