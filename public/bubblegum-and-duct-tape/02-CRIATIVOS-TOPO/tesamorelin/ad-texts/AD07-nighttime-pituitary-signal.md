**[HEADLINE HOOK]**
Why do so many disciplined individuals find that severe calorie cutting fails to flatten a firm, protruding midsection?

**[SUBHEAD — A CRONOBIOLOGIA DO METABOLISMO]**
The answer lies in chronobiology and endocrine signaling.

**[O RITMO DO SONO PROFUNDO]**
Unlike soft surface fat, deep visceral adipose tissue (the dense fat surrounding internal organs) is primarily mobilized during deep Stage 4 sleep through pulsatile bursts of Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH) from the pituitary gland.

During youth, robust nocturnal GHRH pulses signal the body to mobilize visceral fat stores for cellular repair while protecting skeletal muscle mass.

However, after age 30, natural GHRH secretion drops by approximately 14% per decade—a process known as *Somatopause*.

**[AS TRÊS CONSEQUÊNCIAS DA QUEDA DE GHRH]**
When nighttime GHRH pulses diminish:
1. Deep visceral fat becomes resistant to standard calorie deficits.
2. The body prioritizes burning active muscle tissue during periods of dietary restriction.
3. Abdominal volume remains firm and expanded despite weight loss on the scale.

**[O RESTABELECIMENTO COM TESAMORELIN]**
Clinical researchers developed **Tesamorelin** to address this exact signaling deficiency. As a targeted 44-amino-acid GHRH analog, it restores the body's natural pulsatile nighttime endocrine rhythm without the risks of synthetic hormone replacement.

In peer-reviewed Phase 3 clinical studies evaluated by CT imaging, Tesamorelin produced an average 18% reduction in visceral fat volume while maintaining and protecting lean skeletal muscle.

This physician-guided approach targets the root hormonal mechanism behind deep abdominal fat.
