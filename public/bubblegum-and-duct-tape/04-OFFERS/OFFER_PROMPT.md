## Part 2: Unit Economics & Pricing Calculator

### Formula Definitions:
- **Total Revenue ($)** = Retail Selling Price to Customer
- **Total COGS ($)** = `(Wholesale Vial Cost × Total Vials Shipped) + Async Physician Consult ($35) + Reconstitution Kit ($14.95) + Shipping ($14.95)`
- **Gross Profit ($)** = `Total Revenue - Total COGS - Payment Gateway Fee (3%)`
- **Gross Margin (%)** = `(Gross Profit / Total Revenue) × 100`
